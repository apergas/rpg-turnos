# Villaclaro on autoplay

Run every command from the project root with `tools\godot.cmd` (see CLAUDE.md, Commands). Runs that may write `user://` set `APPDATA` inline to a scratch path, never the real one. The numbers below are past measurements: one run varies by about 5 minutes of play time.

- Run the game on autoplay (no title: a new game that walks a route, fights picking commands and continues by itself; saves to `user://save_autoplay.json`): `.\tools\godot.cmd --path . -- --autoplay`
- Whole game so far on autoplay, headless (a few minutes; opens the chest of 25 G, buys the Espada de cobre and the Cota de cuero, finds the Escudo de madera; the Rey Slime comes out at Lv 5, the fog lifts and the route takes the north exit; in the forest it opens the glade chests, buys the zone 2 gear and beats the Bruja at Lv 9, `¡Consigues un Fragmento del amuleto!`): `.\tools\godot.cmd --headless --path . --fixed-fps 10 --quit-after 50000 -- --autoplay`
- One test battle (poisoned Lv 3 hero against a Slime and a Murciélago; quits when it ends; play it with the keyboard or the mouse, or add `-- --autoplay`): `.\tools\godot.cmd --path . res://scenes/battle/battle_scene.tscn`
- Save round trip: `.\tools\godot.cmd --headless --path . --fixed-fps 10 --quit-after 6000 -- --autoplay --autoplay-save` (the `Guardado:` and `Cargado:` JSON lines must be identical). Delete `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\rpg-turnos\save_autoplay.json` afterwards.
- Pause menu frames: `.\tools\godot.cmd --path . --write-movie <dir>\f.png --fixed-fps 10 --quit-after 140 -- --autoplay --autoplay-menu` (no prologue; after the name window, ☰ shows after MENU_TOUR_DELAY 1.0 s, then the main page, Estado, Objetos, Equipo, Diario and Opciones appear every TOUR_STEP 1.5 s).
- Run one session without the title: `.\tools\godot.cmd --path . res://scenes/game/game.tscn -- --autoplay`
- Run only the world map (prints each group touched (and takes it off), shops, inns, chests and exits instead of playing them): `.\tools\godot.cmd --path . res://scenes/world/world.tscn -- --autoplay`
- `--autoplay-zone=medallas` (3.6.0: there are forty small medals and the Coleccionista gives ten prizes; the tour stops at `MEDAL_TOUR_GOAL` 12 medals because 40 take hours even at `MEDAL_TOUR_CHANCE` 0.5 (0.25 reached 24/40 and 0.5 33/40 in `MEDAL_TOUR_TIME`). Headless with `--quit-after 60000`: it hunts the meadow's groups for up to `MEDAL_TOUR_TIME` 1800 s, then talks to the Coleccionista of Villaclaro and the forest village's; ends with `Medallas: 12/40 · premios 3/10`).
- Debug panel (development only): `.\tools\godot.cmd --path . -- --debug`, then F1 on the map (`DebugPanel`: go to any zone, level, gold, items, heal, monster groups off/on, bosses beaten, the village quest's next stage, save). Each action prints `Depuración: ...`. With a saved game it writes your real slot: redirect `APPDATA` when testing.
- The v2 on autoplay (headless, `--quit-after 60000`): `.\tools\godot.cmd --headless --path . --fixed-fps 10 --quit-after 60000 -- --autoplay --autoplay-zone=caravana` (from the finished v1 in Villaclaro through the caravan's arrival, the departure, the Caravana and the jungle) or `--autoplay-zone=jungla` (straight to the jungle with Brena); see `Game` above for what it prints.
- `--autoplay-zone=eco` (Lv 20, platinum gear and helmet, Esqueleto leal, story finished, in the throne room; fights the Eco again and again, puts on the Yelmo de bruma on the first victory and prints each result). Start a new autoplay game in another zone (`--autoplay-zone=bosque`: Lv 5, 150 G, the zone 1 gear, the Rey Slime beaten; it buys the forest gear, sleeps at the inn when below half HP and fights the Bruja at Lv 9, then walks on into the mine): `.\tools\godot.cmd --headless --path . --fixed-fps 10 --quit-after 40000 -- --autoplay --autoplay-zone=bosque`. The mine: `--autoplay-zone=mina_1` (the three floors: shop, chests, lever, key, gate, Golem from Lv 13) or `--autoplay-zone=mina_3` (straight to the Golem at Lv 13). The castle: `--autoplay-zone=castillo_patio` (the camp's shop, the courtyard and its chests, the throne room; the Señor de la Niebla from Lv 17, then the ending, the credits and `Juego completado: ...`) or `--autoplay-zone=castillo_trono` (straight to the Señor at Lv 17).
- Whole game on autoplay, from a new game through every cutscene and at least 3 side quests to the credits (headless, a few minutes of real time): `.\tools\godot.cmd --headless --path . --fixed-fps 10 --quit-after 150000 -- --autoplay`; it ends with `Juego completado: Nv n, tiempo de juego h:mm:ss, misiones n` (Normal, with the monster groups of 1.7.0 and 1.9.0's ambient: Nv 18 at 0:57-1:05 and 4-5 missions; on the mine's third floor a closed gate reached before the key stops the hero, who skips the route points behind it one by one and goes round again (`Player` skips any route point it is stuck on)).
