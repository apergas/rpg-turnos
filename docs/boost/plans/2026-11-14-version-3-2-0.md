# Versión 3.2.0: plan de implementación

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use boost:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or boost:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** La vuelta a Villaclaro y las Ruinas flotantes. Tras el Coloso la Caravana abre la salida "A Villaclaro" a la entrada norte de la pradera, que en la parte 3 tiene la Niebla otra vez en sus caminos y solo la salida "A la caravana"; la Abuela Rosa cuenta de dónde salió el amuleto (escena `amuleto`) y da el Amuleto del Alba. Con él se abren las Ruinas flotantes: dos plantas de celdas al aire libre cuyas celdas de roca son un abismo de niebla (`floating`), losas viejas y columnas rotas, puentes entre islas y la explanada del Vigía; cinco enemigos, el Vigía de la bruma y el Sello de Albor ("Lleváis su luz. Pasad."); el juego de luna y el Elixir en el Herrero de cristal; la etapa 4 de Brumaclara (el mercado abierto, el Coleccionista, todas las ventanas con luz); dos misiones (`cartas`, `caballeros`); fondo `ruinas`, música `ruins`, ambiente de viento; salidas automáticas `ruinas` y `vigia`, y la partida automática de la v3 con la visita a Villaclaro; el héroe sale con Nv 49 en unos 25 minutos. Spec: `docs/boost/specs/2026-11-14-version-3-design.md`, fila 3.2.0 de "Versiones" (M-83 y las Ruinas de M-79 a M-82).

**Architecture:** Casi todo es dato sobre las reglas de la 3.0.0 y la 3.1.0. Dos constructores nuevos: `FloatingIslands` (las islas, sus raíces, el abismo y los muros invisibles; `DungeonMap` lo llama con dos líneas, porque está a 598 de 600) y `VillaclaroLook` (la pradera vestida para la parte 3, como `CampLook`; `ZoneTravel` la usa). La escena del amuleto es la escena de pueblo de la pradera en la parte 3 (`V3Flow.arrival_scene()`, como `CaravanFlow.arrival_scene()`), así que `game.gd` no cambia; un paso `flag` de escena cuyo nombre es un objeto clave da también el objeto. `StoryState` gana `part()`, los objetivos del capítulo 13 (`RUINAS_GOALS`) y lo que Orvalla añade (`keeper_line()`); `v3_way_on()` cuenta también la salida de la Caravana a Villaclaro. `QuestLog` solo ofrece una misión en su parte y antes de su campo nuevo `gone`. `GameAutoplay` lleva el recado de la Caravana hasta Villaclaro. Las figuras nuevas van en `ActorLooksV3` y `MistCreatureBuilder`; el fondo, en `V3Backdrops`; la etapa 4, en `BrumaclaraLook`. `game.gd`, `world.gd`, `battle_actor.gd` y `creature_builder.gd` no cambian.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2 (Compatibility), GDScript, escenas como texto, web PWA de un solo hilo.

## Adaptaciones al spec

- **La pradera tiene una sola salida de la v1** ("Al bosque", al norte). Gana `gone` `brumaclara` (como pide el spec) y su sitio lo ocupa en la parte 3 la salida nueva "A la caravana" (la caravana llegó por el camino del norte). Así nadie se la pierde y no hace falta cablear nada nuevo en `game.gd` (433 líneas, su presupuesto). Después, hablar con la Abuela da sus frases del capítulo 13.

En `scripts/game/quest_log.gd`, `_offered()`:

```gdscript
## True if the quest may be offered now: still OPEN, of the part of the game these flags are in
## (StoryState.part(): the meadow of the v3 offers none of the v1's), its needs flag set and its
## gone flag not.
func _offered(quest: QuestData, flags: Dictionary) -> bool:
```

`scripts/world/floating_islands.gd`:

```gdscript
class_name FloatingIslands extends RefCounted
## Constructor: a floor of cells open to the sky whose rock cells are a void (the Ruinas
## flotantes, 3.2.0; ZoneData.floating), built under DungeonMap instead of its floor and its rock
## blocks: an old flagstone slab on every cell that is not rock (three shades, picked by cell),
## a root of rock hanging under each slab (longer here and there, so the islands' undersides
## are uneven), a sea of mist far below with a thinner layer between it and the roots, an
## invisible wall on every rock cell next to the floor (nobody walks off an island) and the
## ground the hero walks on. root_mesh() also hangs under V3Backdrops' islands. Static only,
## keeps no state.

## A slab: a little narrower than its cell, so the joints show, and how thick.
const SLAB_GAP := 0.14
const SLAB_THICKNESS := 0.5
## The slabs' shades, times the zone's floor_color.
const SLAB_SHADES := [1.0, 0.9, 1.08]
## The roots: as wide as ROOT_RADIUS under the slab (they overlap, so an island hangs as one
## rock), a point ROOT_TIP wide at the bottom, one of ROOT_LENGTHS long (picked by cell).
const ROOT_RADIUS := 2.4
const ROOT_TIP := 0.35
const ROOT_LENGTHS := [2.5, 3.5, 5.0, 4.0, 6.5]
## The void: the sea of mist this far below the islands (the zone's haze), the see-through layer
## higher up, and how far past the grid both reach.
const VOID_DEPTH := 26.0
const MIST_DEPTH := 8.0
const MIST_ALPHA := 0.4
const VOID_MARGIN := 90.0
## The invisible walls on the rock cells next to the floor.
const WALL_HEIGHT := 3.0


static func build(root: Node3D, zone: ZoneData) -> void:
	var size := zone.grid_size()
	var slab := BoxMesh.new()
	slab.size = Vector3(ZoneData.CELL - SLAB_GAP, SLAB_THICKNESS, ZoneData.CELL - SLAB_GAP)
	var by_shade: Array = []
	for shade in SLAB_SHADES:
		var list: Array[Transform3D] = []
		by_shade.append(list)
	var roots: Array[Transform3D] = []
	var walls := StaticBody3D.new()
	root.add_child(walls)
	for y in size.y:
		for x in size.x:
			var cell := Vector2i(x, y)
			var center := zone.cell_center(cell)
			if zone.cell_char(cell) != ZoneData.ROCK:
				var pick := absi(x * 7 + y * 13)
				(by_shade[pick % SLAB_SHADES.size()] as Array).append(
					Transform3D(Basis.IDENTITY, center + Vector3(0, -SLAB_THICKNESS / 2.0, 0)))
				var length: float = ROOT_LENGTHS[pick % ROOT_LENGTHS.size()]
				roots.append(Transform3D(Basis.from_scale(Vector3(1, length, 1)),
					center + Vector3(0, -SLAB_THICKNESS - length / 2.0, 0)))
			elif _touches_floor(zone, cell):
				var shape := CollisionShape3D.new()
				shape.shape = BoxShape3D.new()
				(shape.shape as BoxShape3D).size = Vector3(ZoneData.CELL, WALL_HEIGHT, ZoneData.CELL)
				shape.position = center + Vector3(0, WALL_HEIGHT / 2.0, 0)
				walls.add_child(shape)
	for i in SLAB_SHADES.size():
		LowPoly.add_multimesh(root, slab, by_shade[i], _material(zone.floor_color * SLAB_SHADES[i]))
	LowPoly.add_multimesh(root, root_mesh(ROOT_RADIUS, 1.0), roots, _material(zone.rock_color))
	_add_void(root, zone)
	var ground := StaticBody3D.new()
	var plane := CollisionShape3D.new()
	plane.shape = WorldBoundaryShape3D.new()
	ground.add_child(plane)
	root.add_child(ground)


## A root of rock hanging from an island: top_radius wide at its top, narrowing to ROOT_TIP
## length below (6 sides).
static func root_mesh(top_radius: float, length: float) -> CylinderMesh:
	var mesh := CylinderMesh.new()
	mesh.top_radius = top_radius
	mesh.bottom_radius = ROOT_TIP
	mesh.height = length
	mesh.radial_segments = 6
	mesh.rings = 1
	return mesh


static func _material(color: Color) -> StandardMaterial3D:
	var material := StandardMaterial3D.new()
	material.albedo_color = color
	material.roughness = 1.0
	return material


## True if a cell next to cell (eight neighbours) is not rock.
static func _touches_floor(zone: ZoneData, cell: Vector2i) -> bool:
	for dy in [-1, 0, 1]:
		for dx in [-1, 0, 1]:
			if zone.cell_char(cell + Vector2i(dx, dy)) != ZoneData.ROCK:
				return true
	return false


## The sea of mist under the islands (the zone's haze, VOID_DEPTH below) and the thin layer of
## mist between it and their roots, past the grid on every side.
static func _add_void(root: Node3D, zone: ZoneData) -> void:
	var size := Vector2(zone.grid_size()) * ZoneData.CELL + Vector2.ONE * VOID_MARGIN * 2.0
	var sea := PlaneMesh.new()
	sea.size = size
	BattleStage.add_mesh(root, sea, zone.haze, Vector3(0, -VOID_DEPTH, 0))
	var layer := PlaneMesh.new()
	layer.size = size
	var mist := StandardMaterial3D.new()
	mist.shading_mode = BaseMaterial3D.SHADING_MODE_UNSHADED
	mist.transparency = BaseMaterial3D.TRANSPARENCY_ALPHA
	mist.albedo_color = Color(zone.haze.lightened(0.15), MIST_ALPHA)
	var mi := MeshInstance3D.new()
	mi.mesh = layer
	mi.material_override = mist
	mi.cast_shadow = GeometryInstance3D.SHADOW_CASTING_SETTING_OFF
	mi.position.y = -MIST_DEPTH
	root.add_child(mi)
```

```gdscript
## A mist jellyfish: a see-through bell floating over the ground (it floats like the specter),
## a bright core in it, a frill round its rim, two dark eyes and TENTACLES thin tentacles of
## three lengths hanging below, facing -Z. About 2.3 m tall.
static func build_jellyfish(actor: BattleActor, data: Dictionary) -> void:
	var hover := Node3D.new()
	hover.position.y = data.fly
	hover.scale = Vector3.ONE * data.scale
	actor.body().add_child(hover)
	actor.set_hover(hover)
	actor.add_ghost(hover, LowPoly.hemisphere(0.6, 0.55), data.color, JELLY_ALPHA,
		Vector3(0, 0.3, 0), Vector3.ONE)  # the bell
	actor.add_ghost(hover, LowPoly.sphere(0.2), data.core_color, CORE_ALPHA, Vector3(0, 0.45, 0),
		Vector3.ONE)  # its core
	actor.add_ghost(hover, LowPoly.cylinder(0.62, 0.08), data.color, JELLY_ALPHA,
		Vector3(0, 0.3, 0), Vector3.ONE)  # the frill round the rim
	for x in [-0.14, 0.14]:
		actor.add_mesh(hover, LowPoly.sphere(0.05), Color(0.03, 0.04, 0.12), Vector3(x, 0.5, -0.52))
	for i in TENTACLES:
		var angle := TAU * i / TENTACLES
		var length := 0.7 + 0.25 * (i % 3)
		actor.add_ghost(hover, LowPoly.cylinder(0.03, length), data.tentacle_color, JELLY_ALPHA,
			Vector3(cos(angle) * 0.4, 0.3 - length / 2.0, sin(angle) * 0.4), Vector3.ONE)
```
