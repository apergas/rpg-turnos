# Versión 1.9.0: plan de implementación

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use boost:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or boost:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Que cada zona suene y se mueva un poco más (M-36: un sonido de ambiente sintetizado por zona y partículas alrededor del héroe) y una herramienta de desarrollo para saltar a cualquier punto sin la partida automática (M-38: el panel de depuración de `--debug`). M-37 (el mando) está descartado. El guardado no cambia (sigue en la 13) y las opciones tampoco. Spec: `docs/boost/specs/2026-11-09-version-1-9-0-design.md`.

**Architecture:** Tres clases nuevas. `AmbientLibrary` (estática, como `SfxLibrary`) sintetiza el lecho en bucle de cada ambiente (ruido filtrado, con el final fundido sobre el principio para que el bucle no tenga costura) y sus sonidos sueltos (trinos, pájaros, gotas con eco, ráfagas); `AudioDirector` gana dos reproductores de ambiente con un cruce de 1 s y uno para los sonidos sueltos, que lanza al azar. `Game` enciende el ambiente de la zona con la música del mapa y lo apaga en combate, en los créditos y cuando una escena cambia la música. `ZoneParticles` (constructor estático, como `Vegetation`) hace un `CPUParticles3D` en coordenadas del mundo que `World` lleva con el héroe; `DisplaySettings` lo apaga con calidad baja y lo deja a la mitad en el móvil. `DebugPanel` (un `Control`) solo existe con `--debug`: F1 lo abre y pausa el árbol; cada botón emite una acción que `Game` hace e imprime (`Depuración: ...`). Dos funciones puras nuevas le sirven: `Leveling.set_level()` y `Quest.skip_stage()`; `World` gana `clear_groups()` y un `static var debug_no_groups`.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2 (Compatibility), GDScript, escenas como texto, web PWA de un solo hilo.

## Adaptaciones al spec

- **Una tercera clase nueva, `ZoneParticles`.** El spec nombra `AmbientLibrary` y `DebugPanel`. Las partículas van en un constructor estático propio (`scripts/world/zone_particles.gd`, como `Vegetation`), para no engordar `World` (1490 líneas hoy). `World` solo lo crea y lo lleva con el héroe.
- **El polvo de la mina, en una caja más pequeña.** El spec pide una caja de unos 30 × 20 m y "motas de polvo que brillan cerca del farol". El farol alumbra 10 m (`World.LANTERN_RANGE`), así que el polvo nace en una caja de 14 × 10 m y su material tiene luz: solo se ve donde llega el farol o una antorcha. Las otras tres usan 30 × 20 m.
- **Los sonidos sueltos del castillo son ráfagas que silban.** El spec da "viento que silba, a ráfagas" para el castillo y "los sonidos sueltos (trinos, gotas)". El lecho del castillo ya va a ráfagas; sus sonidos sueltos son silbidos más fuertes, cada 4-9 s. El bosque lanza un pájaro cada 3-7 s ("algún pájaro").
- **Los sonidos sueltos esperan con el árbol en pausa** (menú de pausa o panel de depuración abiertos): el lecho sigue sonando, como la música.
- **Tras una escena que cambió la música, vuelve el ambiente, no la música de antes.** Hoy una escena que pone su música la deja puesta al acabar; eso no cambia. El ambiente sí vuelve (`Game._play_scene()`), como pide el spec.
- **`Ambiente: <id>` se imprime cada vez que empieza a sonar**, también al volver de un combate; apagarlo no imprime nada (la línea `Música: ...` hace lo mismo con la música).
- **Ir a lleva a la entrada de inicio de cada zona** (`ZoneData.start_entry`, la de `_switch_zone(id, "")`): un botón por zona de `ZoneData.IDS`, sin autoguardado (el panel tiene su botón Guardar).
- **Nivel −1 / +1 con una función pura nueva**, `Leveling.set_level(hero, level, base_skills)`: sube o baja de un nivel en un nivel con `GAINS` (las semillas se quedan), pone la EXP justa de ese nivel y rehace los hechizos desde los de `hero.tres` con `Leveling.sync_skills()`, así que bajar quita los que no tocan.
- **Etapa siguiente con una función pura nueva**, `Quest.skip_stage()`: OFFER → HUNT → REPORT (con los 3 slimes contados) → BOSS → DONE. No da las pociones del informe.
- **Marcar vencido un jefe hace lo que haría la victoria con la historia:** pone su bandera y su objeto clave (`GameState.on_boss_defeated()`), el Rey Slime además deja cumplida la misión de la aldea (como su victoria de verdad) y las escenas que esas banderas dejan atrás se marcan vistas (`StoryState.mark_seen_for_flags()`, lo mismo que `--autoplay-zone`). No se juega ninguna escena ni los créditos. Los jefes son los `boss_id` de las zonas (Rey Slime, Bruja, Golem de piedra, Señor de la Niebla); el Eco de la Niebla, que es del final, no está.
- **"Monstruos" dura toda la ejecución.** Quitar los grupos pone `World.debug_no_groups` (estático): ningún mapa pone grupos ni el Slime de Metal hasta que se vuelvan a poner, aunque se cambie de zona. El botón dice "Quitar grupos" o "Poner grupos".
- **Guardar en una ejecución con `--autoplay`** escribe en `save_autoplay.json`, como todo lo de la partida automática (`SaveStore`): el texto dice la ranura igual.
- **El panel también se abre con `--autoplay --debug`**, solo para que el script de la Task 3 lo pueda recorrer: la partida automática nunca lo abre por sí misma.
- **El registro de cambios no menciona el panel**: es solo para desarrollo y no existe en la versión publicada.

- [ ] **Step 1: `AmbientLibrary`.** Crea `scripts/audio/ambient_library.gd`:

```gdscript
class_name AmbientLibrary extends RefCounted
## The sound of each place (ZoneData.ambient), synthesized in code like MusicLibrary and
## SfxLibrary: no audio files. Each ambient is a bed that loops (bed(): filtered noise, LOOP
## seconds, its end cross-faded into its start so the loop has no seam) and short sounds that
## AudioDirector drops on it every gap() seconds (shot(): a trill in the meadow, a bird in
## the forest, a drop with its echo in the mine, a gust whistling in the castle). Streams
## are 22050 Hz, 16-bit mono (MusicLibrary.to_stream()) and cached.

const MEADOW := "meadow"
const FOREST := "forest"
const MINE := "mine"
const CASTLE := "castle"
const IDS: Array[String] = [MEADOW, FOREST, MINE, CASTLE]

## Seconds of each bed, and of the cross-fade that closes its loop.
const LOOP := 8.0
const SEAM := 0.5
## Short sounds of each ambient; AudioDirector picks one at random each time.
const VARIANTS := 4
## Seconds between two short sounds: [least, most].
const GAPS := {MEADOW: [2.0, 6.0], FOREST: [3.0, 7.0], MINE: [1.0, 4.0], CASTLE: [4.0, 9.0]}
## Peak of each bed and of every short sound (AudioDirector plays them at the effects volume
## × its AMBIENT_SHARE).
const BED_PEAK := {MEADOW: 0.35, FOREST: 0.4, MINE: 0.5, CASTLE: 0.35}
const SHOT_PEAK := 0.55
## The echoes of a drop in the mine: [delay in seconds, gain].
const ECHOES := [[0.17, 0.45], [0.34, 0.2], [0.51, 0.08]]
## Samples of fade-in of a drop (no click).
const DROP_FADE_IN := 40
const NOISE_SEED := 777

static var _beds := {}
static var _shots := {}


## Drops the cached streams (AudioDirector calls it when it leaves the tree, so no stream
## outlives the game at exit).
static func clear_cache() -> void:
	_beds.clear()
	_shots.clear()


## Seconds until the next short sound of id.
static func gap(id: String, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> float:
	var limits: Array = GAPS[id]
	return rng.randf_range(limits[0], limits[1])


## Builds the bed and every short sound of id now (Game calls it under black, so the first
## play never stalls). Nothing for "" or an unknown id.
static func prepare(id: String) -> void:
	if not id in IDS:
		return
	bed(id)
	for variant in VARIANTS:
		shot(id, variant)


## The loop of id, computed on the first call: LOOP seconds, its last SEAM seconds of noise
## faded into its first ones, so the sample after the end is the one that followed it.
static func bed(id: String) -> AudioStreamWAV:
	if _beds.has(id):
		return _beds[id]
	var count := int(LOOP * MusicLibrary.RATE)
	var seam := int(SEAM * MusicLibrary.RATE)
	var rng := RandomNumberGenerator.new()
	rng.seed = NOISE_SEED + IDS.find(id)
	var raw: PackedFloat32Array
	match id:
		MEADOW:
			raw = _breeze(count + seam, rng)
		FOREST:
			raw = _leaves(count + seam, rng)
		MINE:
			raw = _rumble(count + seam, rng)
		_:
			raw = _wind(count + seam, rng)
	var samples := raw.slice(0, count)
	for i in seam:
		var t := float(i) / seam
		samples[i] = raw[i] * t + raw[count + i] * (1.0 - t)
	_normalize(samples, BED_PEAK[id])
	var stream := MusicLibrary.to_stream(samples)
	stream.loop_mode = AudioStreamWAV.LOOP_FORWARD
	stream.loop_begin = 0
	stream.loop_end = count
	_beds[id] = stream
	return stream


## Short sound variant (0 .. VARIANTS - 1) of id, computed on the first call.
static func shot(id: String, variant: int) -> AudioStreamWAV:
	var key := "%s_%d" % [id, variant]
	if _shots.has(key):
		return _shots[key]
	var rng := RandomNumberGenerator.new()
	rng.seed = NOISE_SEED * 31 + IDS.find(id) * VARIANTS + variant
	var samples: PackedFloat32Array
	match id:
		MEADOW:
			samples = _trill(rng)
		FOREST:
			samples = _bird(rng)
		MINE:
			samples = _drop(rng)
		_:
			samples = _gust(rng)
	_normalize(samples, SHOT_PEAK)
	var stream := MusicLibrary.to_stream(samples)
	_shots[key] = stream
	return stream


## The meadow: a soft breeze, noise low-passed twice at 500 Hz, swelling slowly.
static func _breeze(count: int, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	out.resize(count)
	var a := _pole(500.0)
	var y1 := 0.0
	var y2 := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var t := float(i) / MusicLibrary.RATE
		y1 += a * (rng.randf_range(-1.0, 1.0) - y1)
		y2 += a * (y1 - y2)
		var swell := 0.55 + 0.3 * sin(TAU * 0.13 * t) + 0.15 * sin(TAU * 0.37 * t + 1.0)
		out[i] = y2 * swell
	return out


## The forest: a deeper wind (low-passed twice at 220 Hz) and leaves rustling in gusts (the
## band between 1200 and 3000 Hz).
static func _leaves(count: int, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	out.resize(count)
	var low_a := _pole(220.0)
	var high_a := _pole(3000.0)
	var mid_a := _pole(1200.0)
	var l1 := 0.0
	var l2 := 0.0
	var high := 0.0
	var mid := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var t := float(i) / MusicLibrary.RATE
		var x := rng.randf_range(-1.0, 1.0)
		l1 += low_a * (x - l1)
		l2 += low_a * (l1 - l2)
		high += high_a * (x - high)
		mid += mid_a * (x - mid)
		var rustle := maxf(0.0, sin(TAU * 0.21 * t) * sin(TAU * 0.09 * t + 2.0))
		out[i] = l2 * (0.7 + 0.2 * sin(TAU * 0.07 * t)) + (high - mid) * rustle * 0.35
	return out


## The mine: a deep rumble, noise low-passed three times at 80 Hz, breathing slowly.
static func _rumble(count: int, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	out.resize(count)
	var a := _pole(80.0)
	var y1 := 0.0
	var y2 := 0.0
	var y3 := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var t := float(i) / MusicLibrary.RATE
		y1 += a * (rng.randf_range(-1.0, 1.0) - y1)
		y2 += a * (y1 - y2)
		y3 += a * (y2 - y3)
		out[i] = y3 * (0.8 + 0.2 * sin(TAU * 0.06 * t))
	return out


## The castle: wind whistling through the walls in gusts (noise through a narrow band-pass
## whose centre wanders around 700 Hz) over a low hiss.
static func _wind(count: int, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	out.resize(count)
	var low_a := _pole(300.0)
	var low := 0.0
	var band := PackedFloat32Array()
	var x1 := 0.0
	var x2 := 0.0
	var y1 := 0.0
	var y2 := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var t := float(i) / MusicLibrary.RATE
		if i % 32 == 0:
			band = _band(700.0 + 200.0 * sin(TAU * 0.05 * t) + 60.0 * sin(TAU * 0.23 * t), 12.0)
		var x := rng.randf_range(-1.0, 1.0)
		var y := band[0] * x + band[1] * x2 - band[2] * y1 - band[3] * y2
		x2 = x1
		x1 = x
		y2 = y1
		y1 = y
		low += low_a * (x - low)
		var gust := pow(maxf(0.0, sin(TAU * 0.11 * t)), 2.0)
		out[i] = 3.0 * y * (0.25 + 0.75 * gust) + 0.25 * low
	return out


## A meadow bird: 2 to 4 quick rising chirps, high.
static func _trill(rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	var base := rng.randf_range(2800.0, 4000.0)
	for n in rng.randi_range(2, 4):
		_chirp(out, base, base * 1.25, rng.randf_range(0.05, 0.08))
		_rest(out, rng.randf_range(0.03, 0.06))
		base *= rng.randf_range(0.92, 1.08)
	return out


## A forest bird: two falling notes, lower and longer ("cu-cú").
static func _bird(rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	var k := rng.randf_range(0.85, 1.15)
	_chirp(out, 1500.0 * k, 1350.0 * k, 0.16)
	_rest(out, 0.12)
	_chirp(out, 1250.0 * k, 1150.0 * k, 0.22)
	_rest(out, 0.05)
	return out


## A drop in the mine: a short falling "plip" and its ECHOES.
static func _drop(rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var rate := MusicLibrary.RATE
	var pitch := rng.randf_range(1400.0, 2200.0)
	var dry := PackedFloat32Array()
	var count := int(0.06 * rate)
	var phase := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var u := float(i) / count
		phase += pitch * pow(0.4, u) / rate
		var env := pow(1.0 - u, 2.0) * minf(1.0, float(i) / DROP_FADE_IN)
		dry.append(sin(TAU * phase) * env)
	var last: Array = ECHOES[ECHOES.size() - 1]
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	out.resize(dry.size() + int(float(last[0]) * rate) + 1)
	out.fill(0.0)
	for i in dry.size():
		out[i] += dry[i]
	for echo: Array in ECHOES:
		var start := int(float(echo[0]) * rate)
		for i in dry.size():
			out[start + i] += dry[i] * float(echo[1])
	return out


## A gust in the castle: 1.2 to 2 s of noise through a narrow band-pass that rises from
## 500 Hz to its top and falls back, swelling and dying away.
static func _gust(rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var count := int(rng.randf_range(1.2, 2.0) * MusicLibrary.RATE)
	var top := rng.randf_range(900.0, 1200.0)
	var out := PackedFloat32Array()
	out.resize(count)
	var band := PackedFloat32Array()
	var x1 := 0.0
	var x2 := 0.0
	var y1 := 0.0
	var y2 := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var u := float(i) / count
		if i % 32 == 0:
			band = _band(lerpf(500.0, top, sin(PI * u)), 14.0)
		var x := rng.randf_range(-1.0, 1.0)
		var y := band[0] * x + band[1] * x2 - band[2] * y1 - band[3] * y2
		x2 = x1
		x1 = x
		y2 = y1
		y1 = y
		out[i] = y * pow(sin(PI * u), 1.5)
	return out


## A sine sliding from hz_from to hz_to over seconds, shaped by sin(π·u) so it starts and
## ends at 0 (no clicks), appended to out.
static func _chirp(out: PackedFloat32Array, hz_from: float, hz_to: float,
		seconds: float) -> void:
	var count := int(seconds * MusicLibrary.RATE)
	var phase := 0.0
	for i in count:
		var u := float(i) / count
		phase += lerpf(hz_from, hz_to, u) / MusicLibrary.RATE
		out.append(sin(TAU * phase) * sin(PI * u))


static func _rest(out: PackedFloat32Array, seconds: float) -> void:
	for i in int(seconds * MusicLibrary.RATE):
		out.append(0.0)


## One-pole low-pass coefficient for a cut-off of hz.
static func _pole(hz: float) -> float:
	return 1.0 - exp(-TAU * hz / MusicLibrary.RATE)


## Band-pass biquad (0 dB at its centre) around hz with quality q, normalized:
## [b0, b2, a1, a2] (b1 is 0): y = b0·x + b2·x[-2] − a1·y[-1] − a2·y[-2].
static func _band(hz: float, q: float) -> PackedFloat32Array:
	var w := TAU * hz / MusicLibrary.RATE
	var alpha := sin(w) / (2.0 * q)
	var a0 := 1.0 + alpha
	return PackedFloat32Array([alpha / a0, -alpha / a0, -2.0 * cos(w) / a0,
		(1.0 - alpha) / a0])


## Scales samples so their loudest one is peak.
static func _normalize(samples: PackedFloat32Array, peak: float) -> void:
	var top := 0.0
	for value in samples:
		top = maxf(top, absf(value))
	if top <= 0.0:
		return
	var k := peak / top
	for i in samples.size():
		samples[i] *= k
```

func set_volumes(music: float, sfx: float) -> void:
	music_volume = music
	sfx_volume = sfx
	if _fade:
		_fade.kill()
	for i in 2:
		var player := _music[i]
		if i == _current and _music_id != "":
			player.volume_linear = _music_level()
		else:
			player.volume_linear = 0.0
			player.stop()
	if _ambient_fade:
		_ambient_fade.kill()
	for i in 2:
		var player := _ambient[i]
		if i == _ambient_current and _ambient_id != "":
			player.volume_linear = _ambient_level()
		else:
			player.volume_linear = 0.0
			player.stop()


func _start(player: AudioStreamPlayer) -> void:
	if not _no_playback:
		player.play()


func _music_level() -> float:
	return 0.0 if _muted else music_volume

	return 0.0 if _muted else sfx_volume * AMBIENT_SHARE

		_fade.kill()
	_fade = create_tween().set_parallel()

		_ambient_fade.kill()
	_ambient_fade = create_tween().set_parallel()

	var size: float = spec.size
	var is_round: bool = spec.round
	var quad := QuadMesh.new()
	quad.size = Vector2(size, size) if is_round else Vector2(size, size * 0.6)
	var mat := StandardMaterial3D.new()
	mat.billboard_mode = BaseMaterial3D.BILLBOARD_PARTICLES
	mat.billboard_keep_scale = true
	mat.transparency = BaseMaterial3D.TRANSPARENCY_ALPHA
	mat.vertex_color_use_as_albedo = true
	mat.cull_mode = BaseMaterial3D.CULL_DISABLED
	if not spec.lit:
		mat.shading_mode = BaseMaterial3D.SHADING_MODE_UNSHADED
	if is_round:
		mat.albedo_texture = _dot()
	quad.material = mat
	return quad


## A white dot fading to clear at its edge (built for each map: no static resource is left
## at exit).
static func _dot() -> GradientTexture2D:
	var gradient := Gradient.new()
	gradient.set_color(0, Color.WHITE)
	gradient.set_color(1, Color(1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 0.0))
	var texture := GradientTexture2D.new()
	texture.gradient = gradient
	texture.fill = GradientTexture2D.FILL_RADIAL
	texture.fill_from = Vector2(0.5, 0.5)
	texture.fill_to = Vector2(1.0, 0.5)
	texture.width = 32
	texture.height = 32
	return texture


## Each particle is born one of the two colours, never a mix (constant interpolation).
static func _pick(colors: Array) -> Gradient:
	var gradient := Gradient.new()
	gradient.interpolation_mode = Gradient.GRADIENT_INTERPOLATE_CONSTANT
	gradient.offsets = PackedFloat32Array([0.0, 0.5])
	gradient.colors = PackedColorArray([colors[0], colors[1]])
	return gradient


## Over its life a particle fades in for FADE of it and out for the last FADE.
static func _fade() -> Gradient:
	var gradient := Gradient.new()
	gradient.offsets = PackedFloat32Array([0.0, FADE, 1.0 - FADE, 1.0])
	gradient.colors = PackedColorArray([Color(1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 0.0), Color.WHITE, Color.WHITE,
		Color(1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 0.0)])
	return gradient

## Debug only (DebugPanel): puts the hero at level (clamped to 1..MAX_LEVEL) with that
## level's EXP, one level of GAINS up or down at a time (current HP and MP go along, as on a
## level up, and then stay within 1..max and 0..max), and with that level's spells:
## base_skills (hero.tres's) and those of `learn` up to it, replacing in order
## (sync_skills()). Seeds and equipment stay.
static func set_level(hero: Combatant, level: int, base_skills: Array[Skill]) -> void:
	level = clampi(level, 1, MAX_LEVEL)
	while hero.level != level:
		var step := 1 if level > hero.level else -1
		hero.level += step
		hero.max_hp += GAINS.max_hp * step
		hero.hp += GAINS.max_hp * step
		hero.max_mp += GAINS.max_mp * step
		hero.mp += GAINS.max_mp * step
		hero.attack += GAINS.attack * step
		hero.defense += GAINS.defense * step
		hero.speed += GAINS.speed * step
	hero.hp = clampi(hero.hp, 1, hero.max_hp)
	hero.mp = clampi(hero.mp, 0, hero.max_mp)
	hero.experience = EXP_TABLE[level - 1]
	hero.skills = base_skills.duplicate()
	sync_skills(hero)

## What a DebugPanel button asks: done at once on the state and the map, printed as
## "Depuración: ..." and said on the panel. Ir a goes on under black (_debug_go()).
func _on_debug(action: String, arg: String) -> void:
	var text := ""

		DebugPanel.ZONE:
			_debug_go(arg)

		DebugPanel.LEVEL:

		DebugPanel.ITEMS:

			text = "+1 de cada objeto (%d objetos)" % Inventory.IDS.size()
		DebugPanel.HEAL:

			text = "PV y PM llenos"
		DebugPanel.MONSTERS:

			if World.debug_no_groups:

			else:
				_respawn_groups()

		DebugPanel.BOSS:

			var item := state.on_boss_defeated(boss)
			if arg == Quest.BOSS_ID:

			state.story.mark_seen_for_flags(state.world_flags)
			_world.set_world_flags(state.world_flags)
			text = "%s %s" % [boss.display_name, "vencida" if boss.female else "vencido"]
			if item != "":

		DebugPanel.QUEST:
			state.quest.skip_stage()
			text = "misión de la aldea: %s" % DebugPanel.STAGE_NAMES[state.quest.stage]
		DebugPanel.SAVE:
			var data := SaveData.from_state(state, _world.player_position(), play_time)

	_refresh_status()

	down.pressed = true
	Input.parse_input_event(down)

	up.keycode = KEY_F1

		waited += 0.25
	await create_timer(0.5).timeout
