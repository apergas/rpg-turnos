# Versión 1.7.0: plan de implementación

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use boost:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or boost:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Quitar los encuentros aleatorios: los monstruos están a la vista en el mapa, en grupos, y el combate empieza cuando el héroe toca uno (M-33). Los grupos vencidos vuelven al construir otra vez el mapa; los que se mueven se apartan de un héroe muy fuerte; el Incienso impide que ataquen; el Slime de Metal anda suelto por la mina mientras se le busca y conserva sus PV entre combates. El guardado pasa a la versión 12. Spec: `docs/boost/specs/2026-11-09-version-1-7-0-design.md`.

**Architecture:** Dos clases puras nuevas. `MonsterGroups` decide dónde se ponen los grupos de un mapa (`ZoneData.monster_count`, cada uno una formación de `EncounterTable.pick_group()`) y guarda las distancias del contacto; `MonsterRoam` decide adónde va la figura de cada grupo (quieta, en su radio, o el Slime de Metal de celda en celda con un camino BFS). `World` solo crea las figuras (`BattleActor` sin colisionador), las mueve en `_physics_process`, mira el contacto y, en la partida automática, manda al héroe a por el grupo más cercano (`Player.hunt()`). `Game` pide a `World` la formación tocada (`contact_enemies()`), le dice cómo acabó (`end_contact()`), vuelve a poner los grupos tras una derrota o el portal (`respawn_groups()`) y pone el Slime de Metal (`add_rare()`). `EncounterMeter` deja de tirar dados (queda `level_rate()`, `LEVEL_GAP` y un `is_shy()` nuevo); `encounter_grace` y `encounter_chance` desaparecen de `ZoneData`. Los PV del Slime de Metal viven en `GameState.metal_slime_hp` (guardado 12) y `BattleEngine.roll_ambush()` no da sorpresa contra él.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7.2 (Compatibility), GDScript, escenas como texto, web PWA de un solo hilo.

## Adaptaciones al spec

- **Carrera del Slime de Metal a 4,5 m/s** (corregido después de escribir el plan, también en el spec): el héroe anda a 5 m/s, así que con 6 m/s no se le podría alcanzar nunca. `RUN_SPEED` es 4.5.
- **Celda de sala en las mazmorras.** El spec pide "una celda de suelo cuyas ocho vecinas también son suelo". Con los layouts de hoy eso da 1 celda en `mina_1`, 1 en `mina_2`, **0 en `mina_3`**, 5 en `castillo_patio` y 18 en `castillo_trono` (contadas en el scratchpad al escribir este plan): no caben los 7 grupos por planta. La regla pasa a "una celda de suelo que está dentro de un bloque de 2 × 2 celdas de suelo" (`MonsterGroups.is_room_cell()`): sigue sin poner nunca un grupo en un pasillo de una celda de ancho, que es lo que la regla buscaba. Con ella, y quitando las salas de las escaleras, el refugio, el campamento, el jefe, los pozos, los cofres, la palanca, la llave y las rejas, quedan unas 21 celdas en `mina_1`, 25 en `mina_2`, 13 en `mina_3`, 60 en el patio y 62 en el trono.
- **`mina_3` tiene 5 grupos, no 7.** Aun con la regla de 2 × 2, sus 13 celdas libres solo admiten 4 o 5 grupos a 8 m (el reparto se intenta `PLACE_TRIES` 6 veces y se queda el mejor: sale 5 casi siempre). Su `monster_count` es 5. Si un mapa no tiene sitio para todos, `MonsterGroups` pone los que caben, sin error (`Grupos: n` lo dice en la partida automática).
- **Distancia para volver a atacar: 4 m.** El spec dice 4 m "como el jefe tras su combate (`BOSS_REARM_RADIUS`)", pero `BOSS_REARM_RADIUS` vale 5. Se usa 4 m (`MonsterGroups.REARM_RADIUS`) con el mismo mecanismo; el del jefe no cambia.
- **Los grupos lejanos siempre se paran.** El spec lo deja para el caso de que el móvil no llegue a 30 fps. No se puede medir aquí, y es barato e invisible (la cámara ve unos 20 m): los grupos a más de 30 m del héroe no se mueven ni se animan nunca (`World.GROUP_ANIMATE_RADIUS`). Medir los 30 fps en un móvil real queda pendiente para el usuario.
- **Cuándo vuelven los grupos.** Además de al cambiar de zona, al volver por el portal y tras una derrota (lo que pide el spec), vuelven al volar con el Ala de viaje dentro de la misma zona (`Game._go_to_town()`, el mismo camino que la derrota) y al cargar una partida (se reparten lejos de donde está el héroe).
- **Calma también al colocar al héroe.** Los 1,5 s sin contactos de tras un combate (`MonsterGroups.CALM_MS`) también corren al colocar al héroe (`World.place_player()`: llegar a una entrada, cargar, el portal) y al acabar una escena, para que un grupo que pasa justo por ahí no salte encima.
- **El Slime de Metal mientras se le busca.** Hoy `ZoneData.rare_chance` (0,25) es la probabilidad por encuentro con la misión en curso. Con el spec ("cada planta tiene un Slime de Metal") pasa a ser siempre: `EncounterTable.rare_chance()` da `RARE_WANTED` 1,0 con la misión en curso y `RARE_AFTER_QUEST` 0,05 con ella cumplida. `ZoneData.rare_chance` deja de usarse y se quita de la clase y de los tres `.tres` de la mina. Al aceptar la misión en el tablón, el Slime de Metal aparece ya en esa planta (`Game._after_quest_talk()`).
- **Los PV del Slime de Metal se guardan al momento.** Tras un combate huido contra él, `Game` guarda la partida en silencio, porque hoy una huida no guarda y los PV se perderían al salir.
- **El texto del Incienso.** Cambia el texto del objeto ("Los monstruos no te atacan durante 200 m.") y también el mensaje de la pausa al usarlo, que hoy dice "Los monstruos se apartan durante 200 m.", para que digan lo mismo.
- **Partida automática con el mapa vacío.** Un mapa sin grupos no vuelve a llenarse hasta que se reconstruye, y en la pradera (sin salidas abiertas antes del Rey Slime) la partida automática se quedaría andando sin EXP. Tras un combate ganado, si no queda ningún grupo y el héroe está por debajo de `autoplay_boss_level`, se retira al pueblo con un Ala de viaje como cuando necesita descansar (y vuelve por el portal: los grupos vuelven). Si no tiene Ala o está en un pueblo, los grupos se reponen al momento e imprime `Grupos repuestos (partida automática)`. Esto último es solo de la partida automática.
- **La partida automática deja la caza** de un grupo al que no llega: si se atasca por el camino o pasan `AUTOPLAY_HUNT_MS` 15 s, vuelve a la ruta y deja ese grupo en paz `AUTOPLAY_HUNT_RETRY_MS` 20 s. Sin eso, un grupo detrás de una roca o el Slime de Metal (más rápido que el héroe) la tendrían atascada.
- **Velocidades.** El spec da los radios, no las velocidades: slimes 1,2 m/s, lobo, araña y duende 2,0, murciélagos y espectros 1,6, esqueletos 1,5 (el héroe anda a 5). Los slimes dan un saltito cada `HOP_TIME` 0,45 s mientras andan.

- [ ] **Step 4: El movimiento.** Crea `scripts/world/monster_roam.gd`:

```gdscript
class_name MonsterRoam extends RefCounted
## Where the figure of one group of monsters on the map goes; World only moves it there.
## Pure: no nodes, with the rng World gives it. Three kinds of movement:
## STILL: it never moves (mushrooms, plants, golems: their idle loop is all).
## ROAM: from one random point within radius of home to another (next_point()), waiting a
## little at each (WAIT); it never chases the hero. Shy (the hero EncounterMeter.LEVEL_GAP
## levels past the zone) it moves away from a hero closer than SHY_RADIUS, at its speed,
## never farther than SHY_REACH times its radius from home. On a dungeon floor every point
## it walks to is on a cell a hero could walk (walkable()).
## WANDER: the Slime de Metal, with no radius: cell by cell (cell_path()) to a random cell
## of the whole floor (wander_cells()) at its speed; with the hero closer than RUN_RADIUS it
## runs at RUN_SPEED to the farthest from him of RUN_PICKS random cells.

enum Kind { STILL, ROAM, WANDER }

## Closer than this to where it goes counts as arrived.
const ARRIVE := 0.15
## Seconds it waits at each point (random between the two).
const WAIT := Vector2(0.6, 2.0)
const SHY_RADIUS := 5.0
const SHY_REACH := 2.0
const RUN_RADIUS := 6.0
const RUN_SPEED := 4.5
const RUN_PICKS := 6
## Random points tried for one it may stand on before staying home.
const POINT_TRIES := 8
## The four steps between cells of cell_path().
const STEPS: Array[Vector2i] = [Vector2i(1, 0), Vector2i(-1, 0), Vector2i(0, 1), Vector2i(0, -1)]

var kind := Kind.STILL
## Where the group was put (the centre of its radius).
var home := Vector3.ZERO
## Metres it walks from home (Combatant.map_roam) and how fast (Combatant.map_speed).
var radius := 0.0
var speed := 1.5
## Where it is walking to now.
var target := Vector3.ZERO
## True if it moved on the last step() (World plays the walk then).
var moving := false

var _rng: RandomNumberGenerator
## The dungeon floor it is on (null on an open map).
var _zone: ZoneData
## GameState.world_flags as World has them (a floor's gates open with one).
var _flags := {}
var _wait := 0.0
## WANDER: the cells it may go to, the cells still to walk (the next first) and whether it
## is running away.
var _cells: Array[Vector2i] = []
var _path: Array[Vector2i] = []
var _running := false


func _init(p_kind: Kind, p_home: Vector3, p_radius: float, p_speed: float,
		rng: RandomNumberGenerator, dungeon: ZoneData = null) -> void:
	kind = p_kind
	home = p_home
	radius = p_radius
	speed = p_speed
	target = p_home
	_rng = rng
	_zone = dungeon
	_wait = _rng.randf_range(WAIT.x, WAIT.y)
	if kind == Kind.WANDER and _zone != null:
		_cells = wander_cells(_zone)


## The movement of a group whose first enemy is leader, put at p_home (dungeon: the floor it
## is on, null on an open map). The Slime de Metal wanders a dungeon floor; an enemy with no
## map_roam stands still.
static func for_leader(leader: Combatant, p_home: Vector3, rng: RandomNumberGenerator,
		dungeon: ZoneData = null) -> MonsterRoam:
	if leader.id == EnemyLibrary.METAL_SLIME and dungeon != null:
		return MonsterRoam.new(Kind.WANDER, p_home, 0.0, leader.map_speed, rng, dungeon)
	var roams := leader.map_roam > 0.0
	return MonsterRoam.new(Kind.ROAM if roams else Kind.STILL, p_home, leader.map_roam,
		leader.map_speed, rng, dungeon)


func set_flags(flags: Dictionary) -> void:
	_flags = flags


## True if a hero could walk the cell of zone: not rock, and a gate only once the floor's
## door flag is set.
static func walkable(zone: ZoneData, cell: Vector2i, flags: Dictionary) -> bool:
	var ch := zone.cell_char(cell)
	if ch == ZoneData.ROCK:
		return false
	if ch == ZoneData.DOOR:
		return zone.door_flag != "" and flags.get(zone.door_flag, false)
	return true


## True if the group may stand at point: anywhere on an open map, on a walkable cell on a
## dungeon floor.
func is_free(point: Vector3) -> bool:
	return _zone == null or walkable(_zone, _zone.cell_at(point), _flags)


## A random point within radius of home where it may stand (home if POINT_TRIES fail).
func next_point() -> Vector3:
	for i in POINT_TRIES:
		var angle := _rng.randf() * TAU
		var point := home + Vector3(cos(angle), 0.0, sin(angle)) * radius * sqrt(_rng.randf())
		if is_free(point):
			return point
	return home


func arrived(pos: Vector3) -> bool:
	return _flat(pos, target) <= ARRIVE


## Where the group is delta seconds after standing at pos, with the hero at hero_pos (shy:
## a ROAM group keeps away from him). Sets moving.
func step(pos: Vector3, delta: float, hero_pos: Vector3, shy: bool) -> Vector3:
	moving = false
	match kind:
		Kind.STILL:
			return pos
		Kind.WANDER:
			return _wander(pos, delta, hero_pos)
	if shy and _flat(pos, hero_pos) < SHY_RADIUS:
		return _keep_away(pos, delta, hero_pos)
	if arrived(pos):
		_wait -= delta
		if _wait > 0.0:
			return pos
		_wait = _rng.randf_range(WAIT.x, WAIT.y)
		target = next_point()
	return _toward(pos, target, speed, delta)


## The cells the Slime de Metal walks to: plain floor or torch cells, out of the stair rooms
## and the village (the refugio).
static func wander_cells(zone: ZoneData) -> Array[Vector2i]:
	var cells: Array[Vector2i] = []
	var size := zone.grid_size()
	for y in size.y:
		for x in size.x:
			var cell := Vector2i(x, y)
			if not zone.cell_char(cell) in [ZoneData.FLOOR, ZoneData.TORCH]:
				continue
			var pos := zone.cell_center(cell)
			if zone.near_stairs(pos):
				continue
			if zone.village_radius > 0.0 and _flat(pos, zone.village_pos) <= zone.village_radius:
				continue
			cells.append(cell)
	return cells


## The cells from start (left out) to goal, one step at a time through walkable() cells
## (breadth first, so the shortest way); empty if goal is start or can't be reached.
static func cell_path(zone: ZoneData, start: Vector2i, goal: Vector2i,
		flags: Dictionary) -> Array[Vector2i]:
	var path: Array[Vector2i] = []
	if start == goal or not walkable(zone, goal, flags):
		return path
	var came := {start: start}
	var queue: Array[Vector2i] = [start]
	var head := 0
	while head < queue.size():
		var current := queue[head]
		head += 1
		if current == goal:
			break
		for step_dir in STEPS:
			var next: Vector2i = current + step_dir
			if came.has(next) or not walkable(zone, next, flags):
				continue
			came[next] = current
			queue.append(next)
	if not came.has(goal):
		return path
	var back := goal
	while back != start:
		path.push_front(back)
		back = came[back]
	return path


## A step straight away from the hero, kept within SHY_REACH times radius of home and where
## it may stand (else it stays).
func _keep_away(pos: Vector3, delta: float, hero_pos: Vector3) -> Vector3:
	var away := Vector3(pos.x - hero_pos.x, 0.0, pos.z - hero_pos.z)
	if away.length() < 0.01:
		away = Vector3.RIGHT
	var next := pos + away.normalized() * speed * delta
	var offset := Vector3(next.x - home.x, 0.0, next.z - home.z)
	var reach := radius * SHY_REACH
	if offset.length() > reach:
		next = home + offset.normalized() * reach
	if not is_free(next) or _flat(next, pos) < 0.001:
		return pos
	moving = true
	return next


## pos moved toward goal by spd × delta at most, never past it.
func _toward(pos: Vector3, goal: Vector3, spd: float, delta: float) -> Vector3:
	var to_goal := Vector3(goal.x - pos.x, 0.0, goal.z - pos.z)
	var dist := to_goal.length()
	if dist <= 0.001:
		return pos
	moving = true
	return pos + to_goal / dist * minf(dist, spd * delta)


## The Slime de Metal: cell by cell to a random cell, or running from a hero too close.
func _wander(pos: Vector3, delta: float, hero_pos: Vector3) -> Vector3:
	if _cells.is_empty():
		return pos
	var near := _flat(pos, hero_pos) < RUN_RADIUS
	if near and not _running:
		_running = true
		_path = cell_path(_zone, _zone.cell_at(pos), _far_cell(hero_pos), _flags)
	if _path.is_empty():
		if near:
			_path = cell_path(_zone, _zone.cell_at(pos), _far_cell(hero_pos), _flags)
		else:
			_running = false
			_wait -= delta
			if _wait > 0.0:
				return pos
			_wait = _rng.randf_range(WAIT.x, WAIT.y)
			var goal_cell := _cells[_rng.randi_range(0, _cells.size() - 1)]
			_path = cell_path(_zone, _zone.cell_at(pos), goal_cell, _flags)
		if _path.is_empty():
			return pos
	var goal := _zone.cell_center(_path[0])
	target = goal
	var next := _toward(pos, goal, RUN_SPEED if _running else speed, delta)
	if _flat(next, goal) <= ARRIVE:
		_path.pop_front()
	return next


## The farthest from hero_pos of RUN_PICKS random cells it may go to.
func _far_cell(hero_pos: Vector3) -> Vector2i:
	var best := _cells[_rng.randi_range(0, _cells.size() - 1)]
	var best_d := _flat(_zone.cell_center(best), hero_pos)
	for i in RUN_PICKS - 1:
		var cell := _cells[_rng.randi_range(0, _cells.size() - 1)]
		var d := _flat(_zone.cell_center(cell), hero_pos)
		if d > best_d:
			best = cell
			best_d = d
	return best


static func _flat(a: Vector3, b: Vector3) -> float:
	return Vector2(a.x, a.z).distance_to(Vector2(b.x, b.z))
```

```gdscript
		if best.size() >= count:

## A floor cell inside a room: plain floor or a torch, and one of the 2 × 2 blocks of cells
## around it is all floor (no rock, no gate). A corridor one cell wide has none.
static func is_room_cell(zone: ZoneData, cell: Vector2i) -> bool:

		return false

		for part in BLOCK:
			var ch := zone.cell_char(cell + corner + part)
			if ch == ZoneData.ROCK or ch == ZoneData.DOOR:
				whole = false
				break
		if whole:
			return true
	return false

## clear of the rocks, the buildings and the trees.

		return false

			and _flat(pos, zone.village_pos) < zone.village_radius + PATH_MARGIN:
		return false

		and not WorldStage.near_tree(zone, pos, TREE_MARGIN)

## figures and, on a dungeon floor, the lever, the key and the gates (CLEAR_MARGIN); the
## boss (BOSS_MARGIN); the entries and avoid (ENTRY_GAP); a dungeon floor's stair rooms and
## village.
static func _clear(zone: ZoneData, pos: Vector3, avoid: Array[Vector3]) -> bool:
	var near: Array[Vector3] = []
	for exit: Dictionary in zone.all_exits():
		near.append(exit.pos)
	for id in Chests.of_zone(zone.id):
		near.append(Chests.CHESTS[id].pos)
	for well: Dictionary in zone.wells:
		near.append(well.pos)
	for npc: Dictionary in zone.npcs:
		near.append(npc.pos)
	if zone.is_dungeon():
		for ch in [ZoneData.CHEST_CELL, ZoneData.LEVER, ZoneData.DOOR, ZoneData.KEY]:
			for cell in zone.find_cells(ch):
				near.append(zone.cell_center(cell))
	for spot in near:
		if _flat(pos, spot) < CLEAR_MARGIN:
			return false
	if (zone.boss_id != "" or zone.postgame_boss_id != "") \
			and _flat(pos, zone.boss_pos) < BOSS_MARGIN:
		return false
	for entry: Array in zone.entries.values():
		if _flat(pos, entry[0]) < ENTRY_GAP:
			return false
	for spot in avoid:
		if _flat(pos, spot) < ENTRY_GAP:
			return false
	if zone.is_dungeon():
		if zone.near_stairs(pos):
			return false
		if zone.village_radius > 0.0 \
				and _flat(pos, zone.village_pos) < zone.village_radius + VILLAGE_MARGIN:
			return false
	return true

	for i in range(list.size() - 1, 0, -1):

		var swap := list[i]
		list[i] = list[j]
		list[j] = swap
```

```gdscript
			tree.pos)

		else:
			single_b.append(xform)

	_add_tree_kind(root, MODELS[zone.tree_models[0]], single_a)

## The trees of zone, in the order they are drawn: {"pos", "yaw", "scale", "use_a" (the
## first single model), "clump", "inside" (inside the walls: it has a collider)}. The same
## rolls every time (zone.tree_seed); the last zone asked about is kept.
static func _tree_layout(zone: ZoneData) -> Array[Dictionary]:
	if _layout_zone == zone.id:
		return _layout
	var rng := RandomNumberGenerator.new()
	rng.seed = zone.tree_seed
	var area := zone.half_size + Vector2.ONE * zone.outer_margin
	var placed: Array[Vector2] = []
	var trees: Array[Dictionary] = []
	for i in zone.tree_tries:
		# Every roll is made even for spots left out, so the layout stays stable.
		var pos := Vector3(rng.randf_range(-area.x, area.x), 0.0, rng.randf_range(-area.y, area.y))
		var roll := rng.randf()
		var yaw := rng.randf() * TAU
		var scl := rng.randf_range(zone.tree_scale.x, zone.tree_scale.y)
		var use_a := rng.randf() < 0.5
		# Distance to the nearest wall (negative outside the field).
		var inset := minf(zone.half_size.x - absf(pos.x), zone.half_size.y - absf(pos.z))
		var rise := clampf(1.0 - inset / zone.tree_rise_inset, 0.0, 1.0)
		if roll > lerpf(zone.tree_center_chance, 1.0, rise) or not _tree_fits(zone, pos, placed):
			continue
		placed.append(Vector2(pos.x, pos.z))
		trees.append({"pos": pos, "yaw": yaw, "scale": scl, "use_a": use_a,
			"clump": inset < zone.tree_clump_inset, "inside": inset > 0.0})
	_layout_zone = zone.id
	_layout = trees
	return trees
```

```gdscript
## After the battle of the group touched (outcome: BattleEngine.State): WON takes it off the
## map until the map is built again ("Grupo vencido (quedan n)" on autoplay), FLED leaves it
## half see-through, starting no battle until the hero is MonsterGroups.REARM_RADIUS away;
## LOST changes nothing (Game sends the hero back and the groups come back with the map).
func end_contact(outcome: int) -> void:
	if _contact < 0:
		return
	var index := _contact
	_contact = -1
	var group: Dictionary = _groups[index]
	if outcome == BattleEngine.State.WON:
		_groups.remove_at(index)
		(group.node as Node3D).queue_free()

func _calm() -> void:

## The hero within MonsterGroups.CONTACT_RADIUS of a group starts its battle (true: the
## map waits for Game), unless it is calm (_calm()) or an Incienso burns. A group fled from
## starts none until the hero is MonsterGroups.REARM_RADIUS away from it.
func _check_groups(pos: Vector3) -> bool:
	var calm := Time.get_ticks_msec() < _calm_until_ms
	for i in _groups.size():
		var group: Dictionary = _groups[i]
		var d := _flat_distance(pos, (group.node as Node3D).position)
		if not group.armed:
			if d > MonsterGroups.REARM_RADIUS:
				group.armed = true
				_fade_group(group)
			continue
		if d <= MonsterGroups.CONTACT_RADIUS and not calm and not _repelled:
			_contact = i
			_trigger(false)
			return true
	return false


## Autoplay: leaves the route for the nearest group within AUTOPLAY_HUNT_RADIUS that can
## start a battle, and goes back to it once that one is gone or fled from, after
## AUTOPLAY_HUNT_MS, or when the hero got stuck on the way (that one is then left alone for
## AUTOPLAY_HUNT_RETRY_MS).
func _update_hunt(pos: Vector3) -> void:
	var now := Time.get_ticks_msec()
	var failed := _player.take_failed_hunt()
	if failed != 0:
		_hunt_skip[failed] = now + AUTOPLAY_HUNT_RETRY_MS
	if _player.is_hunting():
		var current := _player.hunting()
		var index := _group_index(current) if current != null else -1
		if index < 0 or not _groups[index].armed:
			_player.stop_hunt()
		elif now > _hunt_until_ms:
			_hunt_skip[current.get_instance_id()] = now + AUTOPLAY_HUNT_RETRY_MS
			_player.stop_hunt()
		return
	var best: Node3D = null
	var best_d := AUTOPLAY_HUNT_RADIUS
	for group in _groups:
		var node: Node3D = group.node
		if not group.armed or now < int(_hunt_skip.get(node.get_instance_id(), 0)):
			continue
		var d := _flat_distance(pos, node.position)
		if d < best_d:
			best = node
			best_d = d
	if best != null:
		_player.hunt(best)
		_hunt_until_ms = now + AUTOPLAY_HUNT_MS


## The index in _groups of the group on node (-1 if none).
func _group_index(node: Node3D) -> int:
	for i in _groups.size():
		if _groups[i].node == node:
			return i
	return -1
```

```gdscript
class_name EncounterTable extends RefCounted
## Which enemies a group of the map brings (MonsterGroups builds the map with pick_group()),
## from the zone's ZoneData: within start_radius of start_pos only start_formations
## (slimes near the meadow's spawn, one enemy at a time at the forest's entry); elsewhere
## every formation of the zone, with the same weight. The zone's rare enemy (the Slime de
## Metal) is not a group of these: it is on every map built while a quest wants it, on
## RARE_AFTER_QUEST of them once a quest has hunted it (so the bestiary can still be
## completed), never before its quest is taken (rare_on_map()). Pure: no nodes.

## The rare enemy's chance on each map built while a quest wants it, and once the quest
## that hunted it is done.
const RARE_WANTED := 1.0
const RARE_AFTER_QUEST := 0.05


static func near_start(zone: ZoneData, pos: Vector3) -> bool:
	return not zone.start_formations.is_empty() and Vector2(pos.x, pos.z).distance_to(
		Vector2(zone.start_pos.x, zone.start_pos.z)) < zone.start_radius


## How likely the zone's rare enemy is on a map built now (0..1).
static func rare_chance(zone: ZoneData, wanted: Array[String], hunted: Array[String]) -> float:
	if zone.rare_enemy == "":
		return 0.0
	if zone.rare_enemy in wanted:
		return RARE_WANTED
	if zone.rare_enemy in hunted:
		return RARE_AFTER_QUEST
	return 0.0


## True if the zone's rare enemy goes on the map being built (rare_chance()).
static func rare_on_map(zone: ZoneData, wanted: Array[String], hunted: Array[String],
		rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> bool:
	var chance := rare_chance(zone, wanted, hunted)
	return chance > 0.0 and rng.randf() < chance


## The enemy data of the group standing at pos on the map being built (not duplicated:
## BattleScene does it): start_formations near the zone's start, else any formation of the
## zone, every one with the same weight.
static func pick_group(zone: ZoneData, pos: Vector3, rng: RandomNumberGenerator) -> Array[Combatant]:
	var formations: Array = zone.start_formations if near_start(zone, pos) else zone.formations
	return EnemyLibrary.formation(formations[rng.randi_range(0, formations.size() - 1)])
```

```gdscript
## so it is replaced by a copy. Returns the HP it starts with (0: none kept).
func give_metal_slime_hp(enemies: Array[Combatant]) -> int:
	if metal_slime_hp <= 0:
		return 0
	for i in enemies.size():
		if enemies[i].id == EnemyLibrary.METAL_SLIME:
			var metal := enemies[i].duplicate() as Combatant
			metal.hp = mini(metal_slime_hp, metal.max_hp)
			enemies[i] = metal
			return metal.hp
	return 0


## After a battle (outcome: BattleEngine.State) with the Slime de Metal: beaten, it forgets
## its HP; still standing after a FLED one (it ran, or the hero did), it keeps them for the
## next (full HP: nothing to keep). Returns the HP kept now (0: none).
func keep_metal_slime_hp(enemies: Array[Combatant], outcome: int) -> int:
	for enemy in enemies:
		if enemy.id != EnemyLibrary.METAL_SLIME:
			continue
		if outcome == BattleEngine.State.WON:
			metal_slime_hp = 0
		elif outcome == BattleEngine.State.FLED and enemy.is_alive():
			metal_slime_hp = enemy.hp if enemy.hp < enemy.max_hp else 0
			return metal_slime_hp
		return 0
	return 0
```

- **Monstruos que persiguen al héroe.** El spec pide que no persigan.
